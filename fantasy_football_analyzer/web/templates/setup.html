You only do this once. On a computer (not your phone):

  1. Go to fantasy.espn.com and log in.
  2. Open your browser's developer tools:
    • Chrome / Edge: press F12 (or Cmd+Option+I on Mac)
    • Safari: first enable Settings → Advanced → "Show features for web developers", then press Cmd+Option+I
  3. Click the Application tab (Chrome/Edge) or Storage tab (Safari/Firefox).
  4. In the left sidebar, expand Cookies and click https://fantasy.espn.com.
  5. Find the row named espn_s2 — double-click its Value, copy it, and paste it below. It's very long; that's normal.
  6. Find the row named SWID — copy its value including the curly braces and paste it below.

These cookies let the app read your league the way your own browser does. They're stored only in this instance and expire periodically — if the app stops connecting months from now, repeat these steps.
